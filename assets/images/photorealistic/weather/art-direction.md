# verycoolapparel — IN THE ELEMENTS

Four secondary product photographs, one for each product modal. The existing alpine hero is retained because it is not a product modal.

## Cohesion
Original campaign garments and colours are preserved using each studio photograph as an image-edit reference. Adult models wear the apparel; close garment crops dominate the frame rather than distant hikers. Cold slate/mineral-gray environments, harsh sculptural side/rim light, believable suspended weather particles, shallow depth and controlled grain connect the set. The finish combines CG-like cinematic polish with physical fabric, anatomy and photographic realism. Raoul Marks is an art-direction reference; Nike ACG and Arc'teryx are mood/framing references, not actual designs or affiliations.

## Four different cameras and conditions
- Traverse Short / rain: low close wide angle beside a bent knee; cropped waist-through-knee movement on wet granite.
- Contour Merino / snow: steep oblique high angle across shoulder, torso and bent forearm; snow adhering to warm-stone wool.
- Altitude Grid Fleece / snow: low close long-lens three-quarter profile; snow at moss collar/shoulder and tactile fleece.
- Strata Shell / rain: low close wide angle beneath collar; hood, shoulder and chest cropped dynamically with visible droplets.

No logos, text, watermarks or third-party textile marks. Merino and fleece show snow/snowmelt, not a fictional waterproof finish. Weather rendering is creative concept imagery, not a lab test or proof of garment performance.

## Storefront integration
Each product quick-shop modal gets two slides: the first-set floating studio photograph (`products/<slug>/base.webp`) and its new worn weather close-up (`products/<slug>/lifestyle-01.webp`). Manual navigation only: arrows, thumbnails, keyboard left/right within the gallery, and horizontal swipe. Each modal reopens on its studio image. Size selection and add-to-bag behaviour remain unchanged. Existing card images and the homepage hero are unchanged.
