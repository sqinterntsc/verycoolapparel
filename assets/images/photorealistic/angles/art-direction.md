# verycoolapparel — OFF AXIS

One new on-body image for each product, extending the modal sequence to Studio / In the elements / Off axis. Preserve original garment colours and construction by using the exact studio asset as an image-edit reference.

## Camera language
Kinetic freeze-frame energy, ultra-close wide/fisheye perspective, dramatic foreshortening and slight Dutch rotations. Guy Ritchie is a camera-language reference; Raoul Marks is a cinematic CG/photoreal finish reference; Nike ACG and Arc'teryx are outdoor art-direction references only. No affiliations or third-party logos.

- Traverse Short: camera literally below the knees, pointing up at the adult wearer stepping over wet alpine rock. Charcoal shorts dominate, with a practical non-revealing crop.
- Contour Merino: camera directly above the crown, pointing down at an adult looking upward; stone shoulder/torso and bent arms in snow, visibly foreshortened lower body.
- Altitude Grid Fleece: lens near ground looking up at an adult facing downward; moss chest/arms loom, snow and slate sky at the frame edges.
- Strata Shell: direct overhead view of an adult looking upward from the chartreuse storm hood; exaggerated shoulder-to-waist perspective in rain.

## Quality requirements
Angle must be visually unmistakable, not just a mild elevated/low three-quarter camera. Adult wearer and garment fill most of the frame. Natural anatomy despite wide-angle distortion; credible cloth and frozen rain/snow particles. Restrained slate/mineral environment, original charcoal/stone/moss/sulphur colours, tactile material detail and controlled cinematic grain. No text, watermarks, new garment hardware or certification marks.
