## Composition and use
Shared direction: full floating silhouette, upper-left directional light, crisp fabric construction against softer outdoor scenery, premium cinematic CGI finish. Product color, neck/hood openings, hems, cuffs and hardware were compared with the studio originals; exact colors and construction are not certified.

The square masters provide room for centered 4:5 product cards, 9:16 portrait crops and 16:9 landscape crops. Parent reviewed all three crop variants with full garments retained. These are reusable masters, not separate landscape/portrait files. Extremely wide or narrow crops may still need bespoke reframing. Keep `object-position: center` and use `object-fit: cover` for full-bleed frames; `contain` will letterbox a square file in a non-square frame.

Merino and fleece received a size reduction after the first review, and the shell received further reductions/recentering so its hood and hem survive wide crops. Original studio assets remain at `base.webp`; all twelve pre-existing studio and lifestyle WebPs were verified byte-for-byte unchanged using SHA-256 hashes captured before this run.
