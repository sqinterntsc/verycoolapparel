# Alpine hero asset

`assets/images/alpine-landscape.webp` is an edited photograph, not an AI-generated image.

- Photographer: Mathias Reding
- Source: https://unsplash.com/photos/a-black-and-white-photo-of-a-mountain-range--6uN87kZrdE
- Original: 5184 x 3456 pixels, published March 8, 2019
- License: Unsplash License, https://unsplash.com/license
- Retrieved: October 29, 2026

The source was horizontally mirrored to place its foreground rock mass on the left and its sharpest peaks on the right. It was cropped to 2000 x 1100, desaturated, darkened, and given restrained contrast for white hero copy and the storefront's cool slate palette.

The intended generated-image prompt was:

> Premium cinematic editorial photograph of immense alpine wilderness. Ultra-wide 16:9 landscape. Dramatic sharp slate-gray rock peaks predominantly on the right; darker quiet negative space across the left, made from a near-black pine or rock silhouette in the close foreground, for a large white headline. Drifting natural cloud and layered mountain mist. Cool muted dark blue-gray grade, restrained slate tones, almost no green, no purple. Natural photographic detail, realistic atmospheric perspective, subdued overcast directional light, deep blacks, and huge scale. No people, clothing, buildings, roads, logos, typography, watermarks, borders, or fantasy elements.
